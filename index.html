GrokForum watch directory

This is to keep an eye on things affecting all South Africans. Each register is sourced, and drafts are labelled.

  • CrimeWatch SA Draft, live, indexable

    https://crimewatch.grokforum.site/

    Official SAPS crime statistics for South Africa, with civil-society figures shown separately and labelled.

    Repos: crimewatch-sa code · crimewatch-site site

  • PublicFunds SA Draft, not indexed

    https://publicfunds.grokforum.site/

    Budgets, spending, audit results and tenders for South African municipalities and national departments, from official open data, with the gaps shown.

    Repos: publicfunds-sa code · publicfunds-site site

  • MediaWatch SA Draft (pilot), not indexed

    https://mediawatch.grokforum.site/

    A pilot register of bias for or against named actors in South African media, judged only on free public content.

    Repos: mediawatch-sa code

  • PoliticianWatch SA Draft, 269 City of Johannesburg councillor profiles, not indexed

    https://politicianwatch.grokforum.site/

    Neutral, sourced public profiles of South Africa's elected representatives, starting with City of Johannesburg councillors.

    Repos: politicianwatch-sa code · politicianwatch-site site

  • TenderWatch SA Draft, not indexed

    https://tenderwatch.grokforum.site/

    A sourced register of South African government tender awards, in this draft covering the City of Cape Town.

    Repos: tenderwatch-site site

  • Watch directory Draft, indexable

    https://watch.grokforum.site/

    This page: a directory of the watch sites and their public repositories.

    Repos: watch-directory site
